Mini Activities for each country (Japan, America)
1) Find three objects in each country that represent their culture or lifestyle. Done
2) Once found, it show a fact board of those objects.
3) Approach to the landmarks to display the fact board
4) Once they found the all objects and visit the landmarks, they will have to answer a quiz about the objects and landmarks they found in each country.
5) One quiz will have 3 to 5 questions about the objects and landmarks in each country.
6) If the player answers all questions correctly, play SFX and they will receive a reward which shows at the end of the visit
(e.g., a badge or points).
7) If the player answers any question incorrectly, play incorrect SFX and prompt them to review the fact board again before attempting the quiz again.
8) After completing the quiz for one country, the player can move on to the next country and repeat the process.
9) The game will track the player's progress and provide feedback on their performance after each quiz.
10) At the end of the game, the player will receive a summary :
